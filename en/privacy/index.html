Privacy policy

  7. Your rights

  8. Changes to this privacy policy

    We may update our privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with the date of the last update. We recommend checking this page periodically.

    If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us by email: pikover2@gmail.com.
